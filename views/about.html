About Sage Sucre

Meet Sabrina and learn about the sweet journey behind Sage Sucre

Hi, I'm Sabrina!

Welcome to Sage Sucre! I'm so glad you're here. What started as a hobby of baking for friends and family has blossomed into a passion for creating custom cakes and sweet treats for the community.

Every cake and treat I make is crafted with love from my home kitchen. I believe that desserts aren't just about satisfying a sweet tooth—they're about creating memories and celebrating life's special moments, big and small.

The name "Sage Sucre" combines wisdom (sage) with sweetness (sucre in French). It represents my approach to baking: using tried-and-true recipes while adding creative touches that make each creation unique and special.

Made Fresh, Just for You

Every order is made from scratch using quality ingredients. I don't mass-produce or keep items in stock—each creation is baked specifically for you and your event.

I love working with customers to bring their vision to life. Whether you have a detailed design in mind or just a general idea, I'm here to help create something perfect for your celebration.

Thank you for supporting my small business and trusting me with your special occasions. It means the world to me!

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order?

I require at least 48 hours notice for all orders. For custom cakes and large orders, I recommend placing your order 1-2 weeks in advance to ensure availability.

Do you offer delivery?

Currently, I offer pickup from my home kitchen or we can arrange a meetup point. For special circumstances, delivery may be arranged for an additional fee. Let's discuss your needs!

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes! I can work with various dietary needs including gluten-free, dairy-free, and vegan options. Please mention your requirements in the order form so I can provide an accurate quote.

What if I need to cancel or change my order?

Life happens! Please contact me as soon as possible. Depending on timing, we can usually work something out. Note that deposits are non-refundable if cancellation occurs less than 48 hours before pickup.

How do I pay for my order?

After you approve your quote, I'll send payment instructions. I accept e-transfer and other methods. A 50% deposit is required to confirm your order, and the remaining balance is due at pickup.
